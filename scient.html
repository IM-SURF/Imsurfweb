Kinematic interaction factors for rotational response of shallow foundations subjected to Rayleigh waves

Facteur d’interaction cinématique pour la réponse rotationnelle de fondations superficielles soumises à des ondes de Rayleigh

Tanguy Ramanantsoavina, Théodora Makrypidi, Charisis Chatzigogos

13èmes Journées Nationales de Géotechnique et de Géologie de l’Ingénieur (JNGG 2026), Grenoble, France, 7–10 April 2026

Presents kinematic interaction factors for the rotational response of rigid shallow foundations — with and without embedment — subjected to pure Rayleigh waves. The analyses, performed with the Thin Layer Method and validated against the Domain Reduction Method, show that a rotational response exists even for superficial foundations, and that kinematic interaction attenuates the imposed rotation more strongly for larger and more deeply embedded foundations.
