TITLE: Deliverables | IM-SURF DESC: The full list of IM-SURF's planned project deliverables, organized by work package.

Deliverables

Each work package produces a set of written deliverables over the course of the project. This page lists them all in one place; see each work package for its full task description.

WP1 — Project Management and Dissemination

  • D1.1 — Consortium Agreement
  • D1.2 — Disbursement Agreements
  • D1.3 — Interim and follow-up reports of the project
  • D1.4 — Report of steering committee and project progress meetings
  • D1.5 — End of project report

WP2 — Enrichment of Strong Motion Databases

  • D2.1 — Report on the updated strong motion database, including new datasets from recent strong earthquakes
  • D2.2 — Report on hybrid ground motion generation including surface waves

WP3 — Numerical Techniques, Geological to Geotechnical Scale

  • D3.1 — Scientific report on the features of surface waves in alluvial basins, for various source mechanisms and basin configurations
  • D3.2 — Scientific report on the amount of surface waves and the variability of ground motion at the geotechnical scale

WP4 — Seismic SSI with Complete Wave Regime

  • D4.1 — Scientific report validating numerical models for simulating the fully nonlinear SSI response
  • D4.2 — Scientific report on the effect of soil inclusions on the vulnerability and fragility of selected structures

WP5 — Seismic Performance of Large Structures

  • D5.1 — Report on the parametric study of the impact of full “3D” motions with surface waves on seismic response of large structures
  • D5.2 — Final report with guidelines for site response analysis and SSI of large structures, from 1D to 3D

Published outputs are also listed on Scientific Articles, and project news on News & Updates.
