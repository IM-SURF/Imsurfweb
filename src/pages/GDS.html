Géodynamique & Structure (GDS)

Project Coordinator

GDS is an engineering consultancy involved in the civil engineering of major industrial projects and structures that integrate seismic risk, in France and abroad — nuclear works, large-scale industrial installations and bridges in earthquake-prone areas. With more than 40 years of experience in earthquake engineering, soil and structural dynamics, and risk assessment of critical infrastructure, GDS provides the project with its seismic-engineering practice know-how and access to a large inventory of international design and assessment projects. GDS holds one of the largest databases in the world for the post-earthquake response of industrial facilities and equipment.

Role in IM-SURF

GDS coordinates the project and leads WP1 (Project Management and Dissemination of Results) and WP5 (Seismic Performance of Structures of Large Dimensions).

Team
