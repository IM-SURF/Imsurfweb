WP1 — Project Management and Dissemination of Results

Leader: Charisis Chatzigogos (GDS) · runs the whole project duration

WP1 controls the project's overall performance, timing and economic aspects, ensures the consortium reaches its objectives and produces its deliverables, disseminates results, and reports progress to the ANR.

Tasks

  • Task 1.1 — Project Management: administer contracts and the consortium agreement; collect, integrate and submit deliverables; organise internal plenary and review meetings; maintain communication across the consortium; monitor and reschedule the work-activity flow; coordinate financial control and payments.
  • Task 1.2 — Creation and maintenance of the project website: GDS is responsible for design, development and maintenance, with support from all partners. The website publishes the project's description, goals and progress; announces meetings, seminars and workshops; and hosts data and documentation produced during the project.
  • Task 1.3 — Dissemination and exploitation of results: publishing the updated strong-motion database, numerical models and all project documents; journal and conference publications; informative sessions with AFPS and ISSMGE-TC203; a training workshop for practitioners organised with AFPS toward the end of the project. See Impacts and Benefits for the full dissemination plan.

Deliverables

  • D1.2 — Disbursement Agreements
  • D1.3 — Interim and follow-up reports of the project
  • D1.4 — Report of steering committee and project progress meetings
  • D1.5 — End of project report
