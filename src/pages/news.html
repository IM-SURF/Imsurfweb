Stay updated with the latest IMSURF project news and upcoming scientific events or workshops. This page is the single place partners can check for what's changed — new entries are added here whenever the project or the site is updated.

Website refreshed

The IM-SURF website has been rebuilt for better performance and to work properly on mobile devices (the previous menu only opened on hover, which doesn't work on touch screens). Page load is lighter, images are compressed, and the project video now plays on all browsers including Safari/iOS.

Kick-off Meeting for projects ANR 2024

The project IMSURF was presented during the kick-off meeting of ANR projects approved in 2024 by the ANR Evaluation Committee CE22 “Ville, bâtiments et construction, transport et mobilité : transition vers la durabilité”.

During the meeting, IMSURF was presented as one of the 20 projects selected for funding, out of 74 proposals.
