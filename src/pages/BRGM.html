BRGM

Bureau de Recherches Géologiques et Minières

BRGM is the French public institution of reference for Earth Sciences, recognized nationally as a leading authority on surface and sub-surface resources and risks. Its work spans three main activities: scientific research, support for government policy, and international cooperation and development assistance. BRGM has solid experience managing and coordinating research projects under ANR and European programmes (FP6, FP7, H2020), and makes scientific data and methodologies publicly available to help manage natural-hazard problems and territorial-development policy.

Role in IM-SURF

BRGM leads WP2 (Enrichment of Existing Strong Motion Databases for Surface Wave Studies), building on the strong-motion database developed in the earlier ANR MODULATE project.
