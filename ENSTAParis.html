ENSTA Paris

École Nationale Supérieure de Techniques Avancées

ENSTA Paris is among the foremost graduate schools of engineering in France, providing high-level scientific and technological education at the leading edge of its fields. Its six academic departments conduct applied research closely with industry alongside fundamental research, with strong institutional ties to CNRS, INRIA and École Polytechnique that enable participation in larger international research units.

Role in IM-SURF

ENSTA Paris leads WP3 (Numerical Techniques for Obtaining Full 3D Wave Regimes from the Geological to the Geotechnical Scale), contributing its expertise in numerical modelling of basin response and soil dynamics.

Team

Jean-François Semblat, Professor — WP3 leader. Member of the scientific committee of the Association Française du Génie Parasismique (AFPS) and Associate Editor of Soil Dynamics and Earthquake Engineering.

Stéphanie Chaillat, PhD in Mechanics, Directrice de Recherche CNRS.

Also contributing: a post-doctoral researcher to be recruited for the project.
